Two ways to install on Windows (64-bit):

- **XEMX-Setup.exe**: double-click and follow the steps. You choose the VST3 folder (the standard `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3` or your own custom folder), and whether to add the standalone app. Uninstall from Settings → Apps.
- **XEMX-Windows-portable.zip**: copy `XEMX.vst3` into any VST3 folder yourself.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen): click **More info → Run anyway**.

In Ableton Live: Preferences → Plug-Ins → turn on VST3 (system or custom folder) → Rescan.
