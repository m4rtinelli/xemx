XEMX is installed.

Ableton Live
  Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins
  - Standard folder: turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders".
  - Custom folder: turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in Custom Folder" and pick the folder you chose in the installer.
  Click Rescan. XEMX appears under Plug-Ins > VST3. Drag it onto a MIDI track.

Drum samples
  Put your EMX-1 sample folder or ZIP in Downloads\ELECTRIBE, or pick it with the SAMPLES button on the panel.

Patterns
  Drag a .emx file onto the plug-in, or click LOAD .EMX. The card is saved inside your Live set.

Uninstall: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > XEMX.
